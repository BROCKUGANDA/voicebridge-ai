# VoiceBridge AI — Judge Demo Narration Script
# Segments map 1:1 to TTS clips and Playwright frame beats.
# Target: ~3.5–4 minutes. Natural, conversational, no jargon dump.

## 01-hook (0:00–0:25)
Millions of people cannot use a mouse-first dashboard. Blind users, low-vision users, people with motor impairments — they are locked out of the tools everyone else takes for granted.
VoiceBridge AI is a voice-first accessibility companion. You speak. The product listens, acts, and talks back. Built on the AssemblyAI Voice Agent API.

## 02-problem (0:25–0:45)
Most voice demos are chatbots glued onto a form. They talk, but they do not operate anything real.
And when the cloud goes down, those demos become useless — exactly when the people who need them most are left with nothing.

## 03-solution (0:45–1:10)
VoiceBridge is different. A full dashboard you can navigate by voice. Power on, speak naturally, and watch panels, settings, and metrics respond in real time.
One WebSocket to AssemblyAI. One tool the agent calls to read and write real product state. Short spoken confirmations, not essays.

## 04-demo-power (1:10–1:30)
Here is the product. Sign in. Tap power — or press Space. The engine chip shows AssemblyAI healthy. We are live.

## 05-demo-navigate (1:30–1:55)
Say open metrics. The metrics panel opens. Say go to settings. Settings appear. This is not a script — it is a live voice agent calling our secured API.

## 06-demo-prefs (1:55–2:20)
Speak a little faster. The agent writes the preference. Refresh settings and the speech rate is already updated. The agent can also read. How did I do today? You get a spoken summary of the real session.

## 07-demo-fallback (2:20–2:50)
Here is why this is production-shaped, not a demo toy. If AssemblyAI is unreachable, we fall to on-device speech, then browser speech, then typed commands. The product never goes dark.
Plus a real security stack. API keys never leave the server. Sessions are signed. Rate limits, CSRF, audit logs, encryption at rest.

## 08-business (2:50–3:20)
Who pays? Accessibility teams under WCAG and EN 301 549 pressure. Enterprise a11y programs. Voice-first productivity users.
Twelve dollars a month personal. Forty-nine a seat for teams. Custom for enterprise with SSO and phone.
AssemblyAI powers the brain. We own the accessible product surface and the offline ladder.

## 09-close (3:20–3:45)
VoiceBridge AI. Navigate the web by voice. Change settings by voice. Understand your session by voice.
Live demo, open source-ready repo, and a stored AssemblyAI agent you can try today. Thank you.
